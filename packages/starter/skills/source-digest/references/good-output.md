# Worked example — what good looks like

Note four things: the "why I saved this" line is first and is specific, every
quote carries a span, the edges outnumber the paragraphs, and one existing note
got changed as a result — which is the whole point.

Input: a 48-minute conference talk, dropped in as a video file.

# Source — "Why your onboarding is the product" (conf talk, 48m)

```
medium: video
origin: conference recording, dropped into _inbox 2026-08-11
captured: 2026-08-11
length: 48m
```

**Why I saved this:** I'd just written that onboarding was a support cost. The
title said the opposite and I wanted to know if I was wrong.

**What's in it worth keeping**

- Retention curves separate in week one, not month three. Everything after week
  one is described as "watching a decision that was already made". `[04:10–07:55]`
- Their onboarding is run by the team that owns retention, not the team that owns
  support. Reorganised for that reason specifically. `[19:20–22:40]`
- The metric they moved to is time-to-first-real-use, not time-to-activation —
  the distinction being that activation can be faked by a good tour. `[31:05–33:10]`
- No claim that this generalises. Explicitly says it worked for a product with a
  long setup and would not for a product you can use in a minute. `[44:50]`

**The passage that made me stop** `[21:12]`

> "We didn't make onboarding better. We moved it to the people who got punished
> when it was bad."

**What I'll do differently:** nothing yet. It changes who I think should own it,
not what I'd do this week.
